# Product context

The current game is a desktop real-time roguelike campaign set in a ghost-haunted world after the fall of the Three Kingdoms. The player controls 贾苇索 and can configure up to two switchable companions. Exploration, combat, collection, growth and adult character encounters share the experience. Approved rules are in [the design consensus](docs/00_当前设计共识.md); implementation status is in [current status](docs/08_当前实现状态与后续重点.md).

The White Gate chapter has 36 route nodes, 18 configured battles and 38 initial character definitions including the protagonist. The first battle ends with card selection before Emma's story and direct recruitment. Other encounters normally unlock eligibility for later draws; encounter, eligibility, ownership and current party are distinct.

The protagonist can protect the city and also commits serious wrongdoing. The assault on the adult 吕玲绮 is stated without depicting the act; the consequences and her resentment remain. The current chapter ends with a specific truce and limited outside-station assignment; this is the implemented story, not an invented territory-management system.

The next production step is to deliver a clean local project containing the current game, necessary production sources and licenses, with existing campaign saves and settings compatible. Battle balance, whole-chapter play and subjective audio and visual acceptance remain open. Later campaigns, world action economy and relationship progression need separate design.
